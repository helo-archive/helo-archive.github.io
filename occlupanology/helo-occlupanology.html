I specialise in occlupanological data collection through extensive store surveying effort, having 2 years experience in Hong Kong and spent 3.5 years in Sydney (and the surrounding areas) documenting the change in occlupanid and occlupanopsid population of these two cities. I have also conducted short term (a few days ~ a week) expeditions in Melbourne, Brisbane, Canberra, Shenzhen, and Macao. Going forward, I will continue to survey stores in Hong Kong, and I will also plan expeditions in other places in southeast Asia, including but not limited to other cities in mainland China, Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, Malaysia, and Singapore (but none of these expeditions will happen until at least a few years later because HELO has no funding). The occlupanological expedition reports of most of the cities I surveyed are available for free on HORG: https://www.horg.com/horg/?page_id=1220. The Hong Kong report revision and the Sydney report will take a little more time to compile. Be patient.
